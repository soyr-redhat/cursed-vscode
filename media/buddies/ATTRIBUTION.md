# Attribution

The animated GIFs in this folder are vintage web clipart recovered from
GeoCities, retrieved through [GifCities](https://gifcities.org), the Internet
Archive's search engine over the animated GIFs preserved in its GeoCities
collection.

They were made by unknown authors in the 1990s and early 2000s, and were
passed around the early web as free clipart. Their copyright status is
unclear, and for most of them the original author is unidentifiable. They are
included here for the joke, not for profit.

**These files are not covered by this repository's MIT license.** The MIT
license applies to the source code in `src/`, `scripts/`, and `themes/`. The
GIFs in this folder carry whatever rights their original authors hold.

If you are the author of one of these and would like it removed, open an
issue or email the repository owner and it will be deleted immediately, no
questions asked.

To regenerate or extend this folder yourself:

```sh
./scripts/fetch-buddies.sh media/buddies
```
